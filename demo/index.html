01 · Data

Real doodles

15,000 drawings for each of 15 categories from Google's Quick, Draw!, made by real people in under 20 seconds each.

02 · Model

A small CNN

Three convolution blocks learn strokes, then curves, then parts like ears and wheels. Trained in PyTorch on a GPU with random tilts, shifts and zooms.

03 · In your browser

No server

The network is exported to ONNX and run by ONNX Runtime Web. Your drawing never leaves your device.

Data: Quick, Draw! dataset by Google, CC BY 4.0. Built with PyTorch and ONNX Runtime Web.
